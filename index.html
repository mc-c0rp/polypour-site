Privacy Policy for Poly Pour

Effective date: July 21, 2026
Last updated: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the mobile game Poly Pour (listed on Google Play as “Poly Pour: Color Sort Puzzle!”, package m0pxx.polypour; the “Game”), a single-player puzzle game for Android developed and operated by m0pxx (“we”, “us”, “our”), whose developer name on Google Play is mc_c0rp. The developer's legal name and address are shown under “About the developer” on the Game's Google Play page. This policy explains why information is collected, who receives it, how long it is kept, and how you can control it. For the game statistics that the Game sends to our own server (Section 2.1), we are the party responsible for that data (the “controller”). Contact: mc.c0rp@icloud.com.

By downloading, installing, or using the Game, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Summary

  • The Game has no user accounts of its own. If you have a Google Play Games profile, the Game signs you in to Google Play Games for achievements and leaderboards; that is handled by Google (Section 4b).
  • We collect game statistics. Whenever your device is online, the Game sends our server (polypourapi.mwhisper.xyz) records of what happens in the Game: gameplay, in-game currency, in-app purchases, whether ads were shown, and basic technical information. These records are linked to a random installation ID that the Game creates. We use them for analytics, for game features, and to prevent cheating and fraud. We do not sell them, share them with advertisers, or use them for advertising (Sections 2.1 and 3).
  • Our server does not receive your name or Play Games gamer name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, Google account, Play Games ID or advertising ID. (If you email us, we receive your email address; see Section 7.1.)
  • Advertising partners and other third-party components in the Game (Unity, Google, Yandex) collect information such as your advertising ID, your IP address and approximate location, device information, crash reports, and how you interact with ads and the app. Some of these components start as soon as the app launches (Sections 2.2, 2.3, 4 and 7.4).
  • You can ask us to delete the game statistics we hold about you (Section 7.1).

Your game progress (levels, settings, items you own) is saved in a file on your device. We do not receive or back up that file. The game statistics we receive do include some of the same figures, such as your highest level and your gem balance.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Game statistics collected by us

The first time the Game runs with an internet connection, it automatically sets up a secure connection with our server (you do not create an account). From then on it sends records of in-game events (“events”) about every 20 seconds while you play, and when the Game goes to the background. Events recorded while you are offline are kept on your device and sent later. This happens automatically. It does not depend on the advertising choice in Section 7.4, and there is currently no in-game switch to turn it off (your choices are in Section 7.1).

Identifiers. On first launch the Game creates a random installation ID. It is not derived from your device, your Google account, your Play Games profile or your advertising ID, and we do not link it to any of them. The Game also creates a random session ID for each play session. Our server gives each installation a secret key, which the Game uses to sign its events so that they cannot be forged.

What the events can contain:

  • Gameplay and in-app activity: when a session starts and ends, and how long it lasts; levels started, completed or quit, with the mode (campaign, daily, weekly), stars, number of moves, time spent, and hints or extra tubes used; daily rewards, missions, weekly puzzles and achievements; battle-pass progress and rewards; crates opened and what they contained; skins bought with gems or equipped; in-game announcements shown and the buttons tapped; and whether an ad was shown, watched to the end, or held back by the Game's own pacing rules (not which ad or advertiser).
  • In-game currency: every change to your gems and its reason, and your gem, hint and extra-tube balance at the time of each event.
  • Progress summary (at the start of a session): highest level unlocked, total stars, number of skins owned, whether “Remove ads” was bought, battle-pass season, level and premium status, the achievements you have reached, the Game's language, and whether this is the first launch.
  • Real-money purchases: the product, whether the purchase succeeded (or why it failed), the gems granted, the price and currency shown by Google Play, and the transaction identifier that Google Play issues for the purchase. We never receive your payment details or your Google account.
  • Technical and integrity information: Game version, platform (Android), device model, operating-system version, the time on your device, and reports when a game file fails its integrity check, when the gem balance is changed outside the Game, or when the Game has to register again (such a report contains the previous installation ID).
  • IP address: like any internet server, ours receives your IP address with every request. We use it to protect the server from abuse (for example, to limit how many requests one address can make). It is also stored once, with the installation's registration record, for security purposes. We do not use it to find out your location.

The Game also downloads live content from our server (new skins, images, battle-pass seasons, in-game announcements), as well as the share of players who have earned each achievement. These downloads send no identifier, only the IP address and the basic technical information that every internet request carries.

2.2 Information collected by advertising partners

The Game shows banner, rewarded, interstitial and app-open ads through the partners listed in Section 4. To serve, measure and protect ads, these partners and the ad networks they work with collect information from your device. Depending on the partner, this may include:

  • Device and app identifiers: the Android advertising ID, the App Set ID, and identifiers that the partner assigns to your device or to you;
  • IP address, and the approximate location derived from it;
  • Device and network information: for example device model, operating system, language, network type and mobile network operator, and app and SDK performance diagnostics;
  • App and ad interactions: for example app launches and sessions; ads requested, shown, viewed, clicked or completed; and rewards granted;
  • In-app purchase information: some partners (for example Unity Ads, and Yandex AppMetrica described in Section 4) receive information about purchases made in the Game, for ad measurement and analytics;
  • Crash reports and app performance data, collected by the AppMetrica components bundled with the Yandex SDK.

Advertising partners handle this information as independent companies, under their own privacy policies (Section 4). We do not have access to the identifiers or profiles they build. Where the law requires consent, the Game does not start its ad mediation or request any ad until you answer the consent prompt; some partner components start earlier, as explained in Section 7.4.

2.3 Information collected by other third-party components

  • Unity (Unity Technologies). The Game is built with the Unity engine, whose diagnostics may send Unity crash and error reports, device and performance information, and a random installation identifier. The Unity In-App Purchasing component sends Unity information about purchase events: the product, price and currency, the transaction details and receipt from Google Play, device model, operating-system version and language.
  • Google Play services. Google Play Billing processes payments (Section 4a). Google Play Games processes your Play Games profile, including the gamer name you choose, and your achievements and scores (Section 4b). Google Play In-App Review shows Google Play's own rating card; your rating and comment go directly to Google Play, and the Game does not receive them.

If you use the Game's Share button, or open a link from the Game, the shared text or the link goes to the app you choose. We do not receive it.

3. How Information Is Used

We use the game statistics described in Section 2.1 to:

  • Understand and improve the Game (analytics): for example, which levels are too hard, how long sessions last, how many players come back, and how rewards, prices, ads and purchases perform;
  • Run Game features: for example, showing in the Game the share of players who have earned each achievement (an overall figure that identifies no one);
  • Prevent fraud and keep the Game secure: detecting cheating, tampering with the save file or the Game's memory, fake purchases, and abuse of our server.

We do not use these statistics for advertising. We do not sell them, we do not share them with advertising partners or other third parties, and we do not try to find out who you are. Only we and the service providers that run our server on our behalf (Section 4c) handle them. Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on our legitimate interests in running, improving and securing the Game (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to answer you and to handle your request.

Information collected by advertising partners (Section 2.2) is used by those partners to:

  • Serve ads within the Game (including banners, optional rewarded ads and interstitials);
  • Measure ad performance and prevent fraud or repeated/irrelevant ads;
  • Where permitted and where you have consented, show personalized (interest-based) ads; and
  • Support the general operation, mediation, analytics and reporting of their advertising services.

You can limit personalized advertising as described in Section 7.

4a. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

The Game offers optional in-app purchases (for example “Remove ads” and gem packs). Payments are processed entirely by Google Play; we never see or store your payment card details or your Google account. When you buy something, the purchase is recorded in the local save file, and the Game sends our server a purchase event (Section 2.1). Unity In-App Purchasing also sends Unity information about the purchase (Section 2.3). Your purchases remain associated with your Google account for restore purposes under the Google Privacy Policy.

4b. Google Play Games Services

The Game uses Google Play Games Services for achievements, leaderboards and the Play Games overlay. If you already have a Google Play Games profile, the Game signs you in automatically when it starts; you can choose in your Google Play Games settings whether games sign you in automatically. If you do not have a profile, Google may offer to create one, and doing so is optional. If you are not signed in (or stay offline), every feature of the Game keeps working; only achievements and leaderboards are not synced.

When you are signed in, Google processes your Play Games gamer profile (gamer name, avatar and player ID), including the gamer name you choose if you create or change your profile, and receives the Game's achievement progress and leaderboard scores (such as total stars and levels completed). Leaderboard entries may be visible to other players according to your own Google Play Games privacy settings, which you control in your Google account. The Game shows your gamer name and avatar on your device and keeps your gamer name in the local save file. It does not send your gamer name, avatar or Play Games ID to our server. Our server receives your achievements and level progress only as part of the game statistics in Section 2.1, linked to the installation ID and not to your Play Games profile. Google's processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy, and you can manage or delete your Play Games profile data in your Play Games profile settings.

4c. Our server and its service providers

We receive the game statistics in Section 2.1 and store them in a database on a server that we rent from a hosting provider in the Republic of Moldova. Traffic to the server passes through Cloudflare (content delivery and protection against attacks), which processes it on our behalf, including your IP address. Privacy policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy. Our hosting provider and Cloudflare act on our instructions. To run and analyse the server, we also use software tools, which may include cloud-based developer and AI assistants that process the data only to carry out the tasks we give them.

Android permissions used by the Game and its SDKs

The main permissions declared by the Game and the SDKs it includes are:

  • INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: to load ads, process purchases, download live content, and send the game statistics described in Section 2.1.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID: access to the Android Advertising ID (AAID / GAID) and the Privacy Sandbox ad ID, for advertising purposes.
  • ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION: Android Privacy Sandbox APIs, used by advertising SDKs for interest-based ads and conversion measurement where available.
  • READ_BASIC_PHONE_STATE: added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK. It gives access only to basic telephony information such as the network type, not to your phone number or calls.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: in-app purchases through Google Play.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: optional local reminders (for example, the daily puzzle). These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device; there is no push service and nothing is transmitted. You can decline or disable them at any time in system settings.
  • WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE: used by SDK components (for example background work scheduling and analytics delivery) to finish short tasks. They give no access to personal data.
  • Install-referrer access (BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICE): used by advertising SDKs to attribute app installs.

The Game does not request permissions for your contacts, camera, microphone, precise or approximate location, or storage of your personal files.

5. Data Storage & Retention

On your device. Your game progress and settings are kept in an encrypted save file in the Game's private storage. We do not receive, upload or back up this file. The installation ID, and any events waiting to be sent, are kept there too. All of this is deleted from the device when you uninstall the Game or clear its data in your device's Settings. “Reset progress” in the Game resets your progress but keeps the installation ID.

Android backup. If backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the Game's files, including the save file and the installation ID, in your Google backup. If they are restored when you reinstall the Game or set up a new device, the Game continues with the same installation ID. To start fresh, clear the Game's data after the restore, or turn off app backup in your device's settings.

On our server. The game statistics in Section 2.1 are kept as follows:

  • individual events: up to 12 months after we receive them;
  • an installation's records (its registration record, including the IP address stored at registration, and its summary: first and last activity, latest progress and balances, sessions, active days, achievements reached and anti-cheat flags): until 12 months after the installation was last active;
  • records you ask us to delete: deleted within 30 days of your request (Section 7.1);
  • routine server backups: 14 days, so deleted data also disappears from backups within 14 days;
  • our web server's technical access logs (IP address, time and requested address): up to 14 days;
  • emails you send us about a privacy request: until 12 months after we close the request.

We may keep totals and averages that cannot identify any installation (for example, “40% of players finished level 20”) for longer.

Advertising and partner data. Data collected by advertising partners and other third-party components (Sections 2.2 and 2.3) is retained and deleted according to each partner's own privacy policy and retention practices, which we do not control.

7. Your Rights & Choices

7.1 Poly Pour: Color Sort Puzzle! (developer mc_c0rp): deleting your data, getting a copy, and stopping collection

The statistics in Section 2.1 are tied only to a random installation ID, not to your name, email address or Google account, so we need a little help to find yours.

  • Ask us to delete your data, or to send you a copy. Email mc.c0rp@icloud.com with the subject “Poly Pour data request”, and say whether you want deletion or a copy. The installation ID is kept inside the Game and is not currently shown on screen, so please include: your device model; the approximate date you installed the Game; the date and approximate time you last played; and your highest unlocked level and current gem balance. We will find the matching installation, delete all of its records from our server (or send you a copy), and confirm within 30 days. If we cannot identify your installation with reasonable certainty, we will tell you; in any case, its records are deleted on the schedule in Section 5. Deletion on our server does not affect your progress, which is saved on your device. If you keep playing afterwards, new statistics will be collected again under the same installation ID. To prevent that, uninstall the Game or clear its data.
  • What we delete. Every record of that installation on our server: its events, its summary, sessions, active days, achievements reached, anti-cheat flags, and its registration record with the stored IP address. If the Game had to register again at some point, so that your device used more than one installation ID, we delete the records of all the linked installation IDs, including the references to an earlier ID in a later installation's records.
  • What we keep. Copies in our server backups, for up to 14 days; totals and averages that cannot identify anyone (Section 5); and your request email, for 12 months after we close the request. Data held by advertising partners, Unity and Google is not on our server. You can delete or limit it through their own tools (Sections 4, 7.2 and 7.3).
  • Stop collection and delete local data. Uninstall the Game, or clear its data (Settings > Apps > Poly Pour > Storage > Clear data, which also deletes your progress). This deletes the installation ID and any statistics not yet sent from the device, and nothing more is sent from that installation. Records already on our server are no longer linked to anything on your device, and are deleted on the schedule in Section 5. If you want them deleted sooner, email us before uninstalling, as described above. If you install the Game again, it gets a new installation ID, unless Android restores the Game's data from your Google backup (Section 5). Playing offline does not stop collection: events are stored and sent the next time your device is online.

7.2 Ad personalization opt-out (device settings)

You can turn off personalized (interest-based) ads at the device level: open Settings > Google > Ads (wording may vary by device) and enable “Opt out of Ads Personalization” / “Delete advertising ID.” When personalization is off, you may still see ads, but they should not be based on an advertising profile.

7.3 Reset or delete your advertising ID

Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID (or Delete advertising ID). Resetting gives you a new, unlinked identifier; deleting it causes apps to receive a string of zeros instead of your ID.

7.4 Consent for advertising

Where required by law (in particular for users in the EU/EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the Game asks for your consent before it starts its ad mediation and before any ad is requested. Until you answer, the Game requests no ads. However, some advertising components included in the app, in particular the Yandex advertising SDK and the AppMetrica components bundled with it, start automatically when the app launches. They may contact their provider before you answer, sending device and technical information and, if it is available on your device, the advertising ID. Your choice is passed to them as soon as you make it. If you decline, ads are still shown, but they are non-personalized.

You can change your decision at any time in the Game: Settings → “Ads & privacy” → “Change”. Your choice is passed to our mediation partner and to every ad network in the waterfall. You can also use the device-level controls described in Sections 7.2–7.3 at any time.

This consent covers advertising only. Choosing “Essential only” in the consent prompt turns off personalized ads; it does not stop the game statistics in Section 2.1. Those statistics do not include your advertising ID and are never used for advertising. They are sent whether or not you consent to personalized ads; your choices for them are in Section 7.1.

8. Security

Your save file and the Game's queued statistics are kept in the Game's private storage on your device, encrypted and signed so that tampering can be detected. Their security also depends on your device's own protections (screen lock, OS security, physical control). Everything the Game sends to our server travels over encrypted HTTPS connections, and each batch of game statistics is signed with the installation's key. Our database is not publicly accessible. Only the developer can reach it, through password- and token-protected administration tools, including the software tools described in Section 4c. Data transmitted to partners is handled under those partners' security practices. No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. International Transfers

Our server is located in the Republic of Moldova, and traffic to the server passes through Cloudflare's global network (Section 4c). If you live elsewhere, the game statistics in Section 2.1 are sent to, and stored in, a country other than yours. We limit them to the pseudonymous data described in Section 2.1 (no name, contact details or advertising ID) and protect them as described in Section 8. Our advertising partners and other third-party providers are global companies and may process the information they collect on servers in countries other than yours. Those transfers are governed by each partner's privacy policy and legal transfer mechanisms (such as Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable).
